The People Who Inspire Me

Elizabeth Batty

Elizabeth Batty, more known as Lizzie, is a youtuber that does gaming content. Her childhood wasn't all the best, with her mom being an alcoholic and all, but she has been making gaming content and entertaining people online and making people's childhoods despite her childhood not being the best. Asides from that, she's also alcohol free! Proving to people that possible passed down habits don't necessarily have to be continued. She's also proving that you can in fact be a better parent than your parent, because she now has a child. Overall, she's a very inspiring and creative person that lots of people (primarily girls and women) look up to.

Betty La

Betty La is a youtuber that does gaming videos with her four younger siblings. Despite her childhood being quite rough, having to struggle with money problems, her parents arguing, and having to take care of her four younger siblings (and losing the chance to participate in a few things in her childhood because of that), she now makes other people's childhoods, despite hers and her siblings' childhood not being the best one. Her and her siblings also constantly donate to charities, usually donating a lot of things to children and elderly during Christmas. Her and her siblings use the money they get from content creating for good instead of keeping it all to themselves, and a lot of people admire Betty and her siblings for that.

Jorge Rivera-Herrans

Jorge is a Puerto Rican music artist that made EPIC: The Musical. He was actually going to study to be a doctor, but he then stopped to pursue the music career he felt destined to. Him giving up his studies of being a doctor to accomplish his dream of being a music artist has inspired lots of people to start pursuing their dreams in music, too. He proved that something that might seem like a "silly little dream" can actually be achieved, and that became inspiration to not only music and song makers, but to people who are interested in art stuff in general.
